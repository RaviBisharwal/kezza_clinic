What Botox and fillers can and cannot do

Botulinum toxin is very good at softening lines caused by movement, especially frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet. Hyaluronic acid filler is very good at restoring volume: fuller lips, softer smile lines, lifted-looking cheeks and less hollow under-eyes. Neither treatment lifts sagging skin on the jaw and neck, removes deep fixed wrinkles completely or changes skin texture and pigmentation. At your consultation, your doctor explains which of your concerns injections can help with and which need a different approach, such as pigmentation treatment or skin resurfacing.

Dermal fillers by area

  • Lips: small amounts add shape, symmetry or hydration. Results usually last about six months to a year, because the lips move a lot.
  • Cheeks and midface: filler placed over the cheekbones restores volume lost with age or weight change, which can also soften the folds below. Results often last about a year.
  • Smile lines: the folds from the nose to the corners of the mouth can be softened, often by restoring cheek volume first rather than filling the fold alone.
  • Under-eyes: the skin here is thin and the area is delicate. Filler helps hollows, not pigment or puffiness, and it can persist longer than expected, so it is used only after the cause of dark circles is clear.

Safety and choosing where to be treated

Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine, and the dose, the product and the exact injection points all matter. Mayo Clinic lists pain, swelling or bruising at the injection site, headache and flu-like symptoms as common side effects, and droopy eyelids or uneven eyebrows as possible effects when the medicine reaches nearby muscles. For fillers, bruising, swelling, redness, tenderness and small lumps are common and usually settle within one to two weeks. Infection, persistent lumps and allergic reactions are less common.

The most serious filler risk is rare: injection into a blood vessel, which can damage the skin and, very rarely, cause vision loss or stroke. The US Food and Drug Administration advises seeking immediate medical attention for unusual pain, vision changes or skin near the injection turning white, grey or blue. These risks are lowered by treatment from a trained doctor who knows facial anatomy, products from a reliable source, and a clinic that can act quickly, including dissolving hyaluronic acid filler. Never buy filler online or have injections at a salon or a home party.

Tips for your first treatment

  • Book at least two weeks before an important event, so the full result and any review are done in time.
  • Avoid alcohol and, if your doctor agrees, medicines that increase bruising for a day or two beforehand. Do not stop prescribed medicines without asking.
  • Tell the doctor if you get cold sores, as filler around the lips can trigger them.
  • Bring photos if you have a particular look in mind, or of how you looked a few years ago.

Aftercare

You can return to most activities straight away. After Botox, avoid rubbing or pressing on the treated area for 12 hours, stay upright for three to four hours and avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours. After filler, wait until the next day to exercise, avoid sun and heat, and do not press on or massage the area unless your doctor asks you to. A cold pack wrapped in cloth helps swelling. Contact the clinic if you notice a drooping eyelid, unevenness or swelling that lasts more than a week, fever, or any increasing pain.

What affects the cost of Botox and fillers?

For Botox, the cost depends on the number of areas treated, the dose each area needs and the product used. For fillers, it depends on the areas, the amount of filler needed and the product.
