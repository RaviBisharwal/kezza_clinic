What anti-wrinkle injections can and cannot do

Botulinum toxin is very good at softening lines caused by movement, especially frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet. It does not add volume, lift sagging skin or change skin texture and pigmentation. Hollows under the eyes, deep folds from the nose to the mouth and loose skin on the jaw are usually better treated with fillers, skin-tightening treatments or other options. At your consultation, your doctor will explain honestly which of your concerns injections can help with and which need a different approach.

Safety and choosing where to be treated

Botulinum toxin is a prescription medicine, and the dose, the product and the exact injection points all matter. Mayo Clinic lists pain, swelling or bruising at the injection site, headache and flu-like symptoms as common side effects, and droopy eyelids or uneven eyebrows as possible effects when the medicine reaches nearby muscles. Rarely, the effect can spread beyond the treated area. Being treated by a trained doctor who knows facial anatomy, in a clinic that can manage side effects, lowers these risks. Always tell your doctor about your medicines, recent treatments and any nerve or muscle conditions.

Tips for your first treatment

  • Book your first treatment at least two weeks before an important event, so the full result and any review are done in time.
  • Avoid alcohol and, if your doctor agrees, medicines that increase bruising for a day or two beforehand. Do not stop prescribed medicines without asking.
  • Come without makeup on the areas to be treated.
  • Bring photos if you have a particular look in mind, or of how you looked a few years ago.

Aftercare

You can return to most activities straight away. Cleveland Clinic advises not rubbing or pressing on the treated area for 12 hours, staying upright for three to four hours and avoiding strenuous exercise for 24 hours. If you can, wait until the next day for makeup, or apply it lightly without rubbing. If you notice a drooping eyelid or uneven brows as the effect develops, contact the clinic, because some effects can be adjusted at your review.

What affects the cost of anti-wrinkle injections?

The cost depends on the number of areas treated, the dose each area needs and the product used. Treating one area, such as frown lines, costs less than treating the forehead, frown lines and crow's feet together. Your doctor explains the plan and the total before any injection.
