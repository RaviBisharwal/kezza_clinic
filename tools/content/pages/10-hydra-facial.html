Hydra facials for different skin types

  • Oily and congested skin: the extraction step is the main benefit, clearing blackheads and excess oil. Regular sessions can help keep pores clear.
  • Dry and dull skin: exfoliation removes flaky surface cells and the serums add hydration, so the skin feels softer and looks brighter for a while.
  • Sensitive skin: the peel strength and suction can be lowered, and some steps can be skipped. Avoid a session during a flare of rosacea or eczema.
  • Acne-prone skin: useful for blackheads and whiteheads once inflamed acne is under medical control; it is not a replacement for treatment.

How it differs from a salon facial and a chemical peel

A standard salon facial cleanses and massages the skin, often with manual extraction that can leave marks if done roughly. A hydra facial uses a machine to exfoliate and extract with controlled suction, then applies serums in the same session. A medical chemical peel works differently again: a stronger acid is left on the skin to resurface it, which can treat pigmentation and some acne scars but may cause peeling and needs more downtime. Your doctor can advise which of these suits your skin, or how to combine them safely.

A note on names

You will see the treatment advertised as hydra facial, hydrafacial, HydraFacial or aqua facial. HydraFacial is a trademarked brand of hydradermabrasion system; the general technique is also available on other machines. What matters for your skin is a hygienic setup, single-use tips where needed, and steps that are adjusted to your skin by trained staff.

Aftercare

  • Use a gentle cleanser and moisturiser for the rest of the day.
  • Apply sunscreen and avoid direct sun for a day or two.
  • Skip scrubs, peels, retinoids, waxing and saunas for 24 to 48 hours.
  • Leave makeup off for a few hours if you can.

What affects the cost of a hydra facial?

The cost depends on the steps and serums included, any add-ons, and whether you book a single session or a course. Your therapist or doctor explains the options after checking your skin.
