Laser hair removal for Indian skin

Laser hair removal works best when there is a clear contrast between dark hair and lighter skin, because the laser is aimed at pigment. Indian skin also contains more pigment, so the skin can absorb some of the energy. That does not rule laser out, but it means the choice of wavelength and settings matters. The American Academy of Dermatology notes that laser hair removal can be used on darker skin tones but must be performed very carefully.

Longer wavelengths such as 1064 nm Nd:YAG pass through the skin's surface pigment more easily and are generally considered the safer option for darker skin. A triple-wavelength laser lets the team combine wavelengths and adjust energy for your skin type and the thickness of your hair, while contact cooling protects the surface. Staying out of the sun before and during your course lowers the risk of burns and pigment changes.

How to prepare for laser hair removal

  • Avoid sun tanning and use a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) on exposed areas.
  • Stop waxing, plucking, threading and electrolysis on the area for about 4 weeks before treatment, because laser needs the hair root in place. Shaving is fine.
  • Shave the area the day before your appointment.
  • Come with clean skin, without creams, deodorant or makeup on the area.
  • Tell the team about any medicines, recent sun exposure or skin conditions.

Side effects and safety

Mild redness, swelling and a warm feeling are common and usually fade within a few hours. Uncommon side effects include blistering, crusting, scarring and changes in skin colour or texture, which is why the right settings, eye protection and a trained team are essential. Follow your aftercare advice: avoid sun, hot baths and saunas for a few days, and use sunscreen on treated skin.

Why laser cannot remove white hair

White and grey hairs have little or no melanin, so the laser has nothing to target and the follicle is not damaged. This is true for every laser type. For white, grey, blonde or red hair, electrolysis treats each follicle directly, whatever its colour.
