GFC vs PRP: what is the difference?

Both treatments deliver growth factors from your own blood to thinning follicles. The difference is in the preparation and in what is injected.

What is injectedGrowth factors released from platelets, without the cellsConcentrated platelets in plasma

What does the research say?

A randomised controlled trial published in 2026 in the Journal of Cutaneous and Aesthetic Surgery compared GFC with PRP in 44 people with pattern hair loss who were also using 5% minoxidil. After three monthly sessions, hair improvement was similar in both groups. The clear difference was comfort: the median pain score was 2 out of 10 with GFC and 6 out of 10 with PRP, and satisfaction was higher with GFC. The authors note that the study was small and the follow-up short.

A 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis in Aesthetic Plastic Surgery, covering 12 studies and 745 patients, found that growth-factor injections appear safe and improve hair density, thickness and patient satisfaction over 12 months. It also warned that the studies used different methods and several had a high risk of bias, so the evidence is promising rather than settled.

GFC works best as part of a plan

GFC is one part of hair-loss treatment, not a replacement for a diagnosis. Your doctor may combine it with medicines such as minoxidil or finasteride, check for problems like low iron or thyroid imbalance, and suggest changes to scalp care. If some areas have already gone bald, a hair transplant can restore them, while GFC helps protect the hair around them.

Side effects and aftercare

Most people have only mild tenderness, redness or slight swelling for a day. Small bruises at injection sites are possible. On the day of the session, avoid touching the scalp more than necessary and follow your doctor's advice on washing, hair products and exercise. Tell your doctor in advance about any blood-thinning medicines or bleeding problems.

What affects the cost of GFC?

GFC kits usually cost more per session than PRP, while PRP may need more sessions, so it is worth comparing the whole course rather than a single session. The number of sessions you need depends on your stage of hair loss and how you respond. Your doctor recommends a plan after examining your scalp.
