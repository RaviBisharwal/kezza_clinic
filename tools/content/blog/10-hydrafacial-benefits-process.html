The short answer

A HydraFacial is a non-invasive, multi-step medical treatment that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts impurities and infuses hydrating serums into the skin using patented vortex suction. Unlike conventional manual facials that can irritate the skin, HydraFacial uses gentle lactic, glycolic and salicylic acids to loosen debris while vacuuming out clogged pores, followed by intensive peptide and antioxidant hydration. The treatment takes 30 to 45 minutes with zero downtime.

How Vortex-Fusion technology works

The core innovation behind genuine HydraFacial devices is the HydroPeel® tip. Featuring a patented spiral design, the tip creates a fluid vortex when placed against the skin.

As the device glides over the epidermal surface, it simultaneously delivers a continuous flow of clinical solutions while drawing impurities out through a closed-loop pneumatic suction tube. This eliminates the pinching, tissue bruising and capillary trauma commonly associated with manual blackhead extraction tools.

Proven clinical benefits of HydraFacial

  • Targeted pore decongestion: Salicylic acid penetrates lipophilic sebaceous ducts to dissolve oxidized sebum plugs (blackheads) and dead cellular debris without manual squeezing.
  • Enhanced barrier hydration: Micro-droplets of hyaluronic acid are driven into the upper epidermal layers, noticeably improving skin suppleness and softening fine dehydration lines.
  • Uniform skin tone & texture: By clearing the rough stratum corneum and infusing antioxidants, light reflects evenly across the facial plane, producing an immediate healthy radiance.
  • Safe across skin types: The absence of thermal heat or mechanical micro-wounds makes HydraFacial well-tolerated on Fitzpatrick skin types I through VI without thermal hyperpigmentation risk.

Who is a good candidate?

HydraFacial is suitable for both men and women experiencing:

  • Dull, fatigued or dehydrated skin caused by urban pollution and sun exposure.
  • Congested T-zones, enlarged visible pores, blackheads and oily skin.
  • Uneven skin texture or mild hyperpigmentation.
  • Pre-event skin preparation when immediate radiance is desired without peeling.

When should HydraFacial be delayed?

A responsible dermatologist will postpone HydraFacial in the presence of:

  • Active cystic or pustular acne flare-ups: Vacuum suction over inflamed pustules can spread bacterial exudate and exacerbate inflammation. Active breakouts should first be controlled with medical anti-acne therapy.
  • Active viral infections (Herpes simplex / cold sores): Mechanical action can spread viral lesions across the perioral area.
  • Severe active eczema, psoriasis or sunburn: Impaired epidermal barrier integrity must heal before chemical exfoliation.

Medical HydraFacial vs salon facials

Standard beauty salon facials often utilize hot facial steam (which can trigger melasma in pigmentation-prone skin), aggressive walnut or apricot physical scrubs, and manual metal extractors. In contrast, clinic-grade HydraFacial at Kezza is calibrated by trained aesthetic physicians using medical concentrations of AHA/BHA solutions and closed-loop sanitary disposables for every patient.

Post-treatment care tips

To preserve your treatment glow and protect freshly exfoliated skin:

  1. Avoid washing your face for at least 4 to 6 hours after treatment to allow infused peptides and hyaluronic acid to fully absorb.
  2. Refrain from applying heavy makeup or perfumed foundations for 24 hours.
  3. Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 50) daily and avoid direct sun exposure.
  4. Pause strong active topicals (such as tretinoin, retinol, glycolic acid or benzoyl peroxide) for 48 hours following your session.
