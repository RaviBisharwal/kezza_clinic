FUE Hair Transplant — Precision, Density & Zero-Scar Technique

What is FUE?

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is one of the world's most advanced hair restoration techniques. FUE leaves no linear scar, ensures minimal downtime, and delivers highly natural results that blend perfectly with your natural hairline.

Benefits of FUE

  • No linear scars
  • Minimal pain
  • Fast recovery
  • High customization
  • Natural, progressive growth
  • Perfect for frontal, crown & temple areas

FUE at Kezza — What Makes Us Advanced

  • Sapphire micro-incisions
  • Digital density planning
  • High graft survival rate
  • Sterile surgical environment

DHI — Direct Hair Implantation for Ultra-Precise Results

What is DHI?

DHI uses Choi Implanter Pens for direct implantation without creating pre-made incisions, resulting in:

  • Perfect angle control
  • Higher density
  • Less trauma
  • Faster healing

Benefits of DHI

  • No incisions
  • No scars
  • Maximum density
  • Ideal for hairline & frontal zones
  • Fastest recovery

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hair transplant painful? +

Hair transplant procedures are performed under local anesthesia, making them virtually painless. Most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after the procedure.

Will results look natural? +

Yes, our advanced techniques ensure completely natural-looking results that blend seamlessly with your existing hair.

When will hair grow? +

New hair growth typically begins 3-4 months after the procedure, with full results visible after 12-18 months.

Recovery time? +

Most patients can return to work within 2-3 days. Full recovery typically takes 7-10 days.

How many grafts do I need? +

The number of grafts depends on your specific hair loss pattern and desired density. Our consultation will determine the exact number needed.
