Frequently Asked Questions

Are laser skin treatments painful? +

Most skin and laser procedures at Kezza Clinic are virtually painless. We utilize sub-zero contact cooling tips (-5°C) and clinical topical anesthetic creams to ensure complete comfort throughout your procedure.

How soon can I see results after a skin treatment? +

Treatments like HydraFacial MD deliver instant pore clearance and radiant glow right after the 60-minute session. Laser toning and acne scar remodeling typically show progressive improvement over 2 to 4 weeks as new dermal collagen regenerates.

Will my skin peel or become red after chemical peels? +

Mild micro-flaking can occur between days 3 to 5 following medium peels. This is a healthy indication of accelerated stratum corneum turnover, revealing fresh, unblemished skin underneath.

Are laser devices safe for darker Indian skin tones? +

Yes. All laser systems at Kezza Clinic are US FDA-approved and specifically calibrated with 1064nm long-pulse and Nd:YAG wavelengths to safely bypass epidermal melanin, eliminating any risk of post-inflammatory burns.

Can I resume makeup and gym workouts immediately? +

For HydraFacial MD and mild facials, mineral makeup can be worn within 4 to 6 hours. For fractional laser or subcision, we advise waiting 24 to 48 hours and avoiding heavy sweating to protect the healing micro-channels.
