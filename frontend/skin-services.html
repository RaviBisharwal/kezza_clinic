What is Hydra Facial?

Hydra Facial is one of the world's most popular dermatology treatments designed to deep-clean pores, exfoliate dead skin, extract impurities, and infuse nourishing serums — all in a single session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are treatments painful? +

Most skin treatments involve minimal discomfort. We use topical anesthetics and cooling techniques to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

How long until results appear? +

Results vary by treatment. Some like Hydra Facial show immediate results, while others like laser treatments may take 2-4 weeks to show full effects.

Will my skin peel after treatment? +

Some treatments like chemical peels may cause mild peeling for 3-5 days. This is normal and indicates the treatment is working effectively.

Can I wear makeup after treatment? +

For most treatments, you can apply makeup after 24 hours. For intensive procedures, we recommend waiting 48-72 hours.

Are lasers safe for Indian skin? +

Yes, our advanced laser systems are specifically calibrated for Indian skin types and are completely safe when performed by our trained specialists.
