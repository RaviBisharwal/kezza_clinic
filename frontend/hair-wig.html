Frequently Asked Questions

Hair System & Wig FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about non-surgical hair replacement in Jaipur.

Can I shower, swim, and exercise with a non-surgical hair system?
Yes. When secured with medical-grade waterproof bio-bonding adhesive or high-tack dermal tape, you can swim, shower, exercise, and shampoo normally. Kezza Clinic specialists provide personalized water-resistant styling and maintenance advice.
Will anyone be able to tell that I am wearing a hair patch or wig?
No. Our hair systems use ultra-fine Swiss micro-lace or thin poly bio-skin bases with single-hair hand-tied ventilation. The front hairline features irregular micro-contours that mimic natural scalp follicle emergence with undetectable realism.
How often does a bonded hair system need professional maintenance?
Full-bond systems typically require maintenance every 3 to 4 weeks. During this brief visit, the system is detached, your natural scalp is exfoliated and shampooed, and the patch is cleaned, re-conditioned, and re-secured with fresh medical adhesive.
What is the difference between a synthetic wig and a natural hair system?
Synthetic wigs use plastic fibers that look unnatural under sunlight, tangle easily, and cannot tolerate heat styling. Kezza Clinic systems are crafted exclusively from pure natural virgin human hair tailored to your exact hair color, curl pattern, and density.
Is non-surgical hair replacement safe for sensitive scalps and alopecia?
Yes. Our bases use medical-grade, hypoallergenic, breathable materials that prevent sweat accumulation and allow your scalp to breathe freely. Our dermatologists conduct preliminary patch assessments for sensitive skin.
