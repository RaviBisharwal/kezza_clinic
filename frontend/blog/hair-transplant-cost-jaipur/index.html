Hair Transplant Cost in Jaipur: What Determines the Price

Two quotes for a "hair transplant" can be very different. Here is what actually drives the price, and what to check before you compare.

By the Kezza Hair & Skin Clinic team Updated 22 September 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

  • The cost of a hair transplant mainly follows the number of grafts you need, which depends on the area to cover, your hair-loss stage and the density you want.
  • The technique (FUE, DHI or FUT), your donor area and whether you need one or two sessions also change the estimate.
  • A much lower quote can mean fewer grafts, technician-led surgery or missing aftercare, so always ask what is included.
  • Kezza gives a written estimate only after examining your scalp, and EMI plans are available on most procedures.

The short answer

There is no single price for a hair transplant, because no two cases need the same amount of work. The biggest factor is the number of grafts needed to cover your thinning area at a natural density. After that come the technique, the quality of your donor area, whether the work fits in one session, who performs the surgery and what aftercare is included. A trustworthy estimate needs a scalp examination first, which is why Kezza gives a written estimate only after your consultation.

1. How many grafts you need

A graft is a follicular unit: a natural group of one to four hairs. Most clinics in India price by the graft or by a package built on a graft count, so the size of the area you want covered drives the cost more than anything else. The number rises with the stage of hair loss and the density you are aiming for.

Surgeons use rough ranges like these as a starting point for men with pattern hair loss:

Norwood stageWhat is usually treatedApproximate grafts
Stage 2Temple corners; surgery is rarely recommended yet0 to 1,500
Stage 3Hairline and templesAbout 1,500 to 2,500
Stage 4Front, mid-scalp and early crownAbout 2,500 to 3,500
Stage 5Front to crown; often planned in two sessionsAbout 3,500 to 4,500+
Stages 6 and 7Large areas where donor supply sets the limitAbout 4,000 to 6,000+

These are approximations. Your actual number depends on donor density, hair thickness, curl, scalp size and the look you want. Not sure of your stage? See our guide to the Norwood scale.

2. Technique: FUE, DHI or FUT

All three move your own follicles, but they use different amounts of surgical time. DHI implants every graft with a pen-like implanter, which is slower per graft and needs more team time, so many clinics price it higher than standard FUE. FUE with pre-made recipient sites is usually more efficient for large sessions. FUT (strip surgery) harvests grafts differently and leaves a fine linear scar. Read FUE vs DHI: which is right for you? for the differences.

3. Your donor area and hair characteristics

A dense donor area with thick hair gives more coverage per graft, so fewer grafts may achieve the look you want. Fine hair, low donor density or a high contrast between hair and skin colour can mean more grafts for the same visual result. In some cases, beard or body hair can add donor supply, which changes the plan and the cost.

4. One session or two

Large cases are often safer and more natural when split into two sessions several months apart. That spreads the cost, but the total may be higher than a single-session quote that promises the same coverage in one day. Be cautious of any quote that offers very large graft numbers in one sitting without checking whether your donor area can support it.

5. Who performs the surgery, and where

Hair transplantation is surgery. The ISHRS has warned about "black-market" clinics where unqualified technicians do the operation, and lists outcomes such as scarring, unnatural hairlines, poor growth, depleted donor areas and patients receiving far fewer grafts than they paid for. Doctor-led surgery in a sterile operation theatre costs more to run, and that is part of what you are paying for.

6. What is included

When you compare quotes, check whether each one includes:

  • the consultation and trichoscopy (scalp examination under magnification);
  • local anaesthesia, medicines and an aftercare kit;
  • follow-up reviews (Kezza reviews patients at 10 days, 1, 3, 6 and 12 months);
  • any PRP or GFC sessions recommended alongside the transplant;
  • medical therapy to protect your existing hair, which many doctors advise because native hair can keep thinning.

Red flags when comparing quotes

  • A final price given on the phone or WhatsApp without anyone examining your scalp.
  • "Unlimited grafts" or a graft count far above what your donor area could safely supply.
  • No named doctor, or no answer when you ask who will perform each step.
  • Pressure to book today to keep a discount.
  • No written plan listing the graft count, technique and what is included.
  • No follow-up after surgery.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a hair transplant cost in Jaipur?
It depends mainly on how many grafts you need, which technique is used and whether one or two sessions are required. That is why a reliable price can only be given after a doctor has examined your scalp and donor area. At Kezza you receive a written estimate after your consultation, before you commit.
Is a cheaper hair transplant worth it?
Only if you are comparing the same thing: the same graft count, a named doctor performing the surgery, the same technique and the same aftercare. A low price that hides fewer grafts, technician-led surgery or no follow-up usually costs more in the long run, especially if a repair is needed.
Is a hair transplant priced per graft or per session?
Many clinics in India quote per graft, while others quote a package per session. Either way, ask for the planned graft count in writing and what the price includes, so you can compare quotes fairly.
Are EMI options available?
Yes. EMI plans are available on most procedures at Kezza. Ask the team about current options when you book your consultation.
Does the price include medicines or PRP?
It varies from clinic to clinic. Ask whether post-operative medicines, the aftercare kit, follow-up reviews and any PRP or GFC sessions are included, and have them listed in your written estimate.
