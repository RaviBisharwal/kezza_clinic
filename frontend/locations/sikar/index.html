What we treat

Hair and skin treatments in Sikar

Our Sikar doctors also practise at our Jaipur clinic, so you get the same protocols closer to home.

Hair transplant in Sikar

FUE hair transplant consultations and procedures with Dr. Ankit Bhalothia, who also sees patients in Jaipur. Beard and eyebrow transplants can be discussed at the same visit.

PRP and GFC hair treatment

For hair fall and early thinning, PRP and GFC injections are available in Sikar, after a scalp check to find the cause.

Laser hair removal in Sikar

Laser hair removal for the face, underarms, arms, legs and body, with settings chosen for your skin type.

Acne, pigmentation and facials

Dr. Neelam Choudhary, aesthetic physician and skin specialist, treats acne and scars and pigmentation. Hydra facials and medical facials are also offered.

Body sculpting and permanent makeup

Non-surgical body sculpting, and permanent makeup for brows, lips and eyeliner with our PMU artist, Krishna Choudhary.

First visit

Your first visit to Kezza Sikar

01

Book a slot

Call +91-9284517427 or WhatsApp the clinic with your concern and a preferred time. The team confirms which doctor you will see.

02

Bring your history

Bring previous prescriptions, reports and a list of the medicines you take. For a hair assessment, come with clean, dry hair without oil or styling products.

03

Consultation and examination

The doctor listens to your concerns and examines your scalp or skin, using trichoscopy for hair loss.

04

A clear plan

You receive a treatment plan with the number of sessions or grafts and an estimate, so you can decide in your own time.

FAQs

Visiting our Sikar clinic

What are the timings of Kezza Clinic Sikar?
The Sikar clinic is open from 9 AM to 8 PM, all 7 days a week, including Sundays. Booking ahead by phone or WhatsApp makes sure the right doctor is available when you arrive.
Where is Kezza Clinic in Sikar?
1st Floor, Shakambhari Heights, Opp. S.K. Hospital, Silver Jubilee Road, Sikar, Rajasthan 332001. The clinic is on the first floor of Shakambhari Heights on Silver Jubilee Road, directly opposite S.K. Hospital. Use Google Maps directions to reach the door.
Which doctors see patients at the Sikar clinic?
Dr. Ankit Bhalothia, Dr. Neelam Choudhary, Krishna Choudhary. See their qualifications and experience on our doctors page.
How do I book an appointment at the Sikar clinic?
Call +91-9284517427 or +91-7073565197, message us on WhatsApp, or use the form on our contact page. Share your concern and a preferred time, and the team will confirm your slot.
Can I get a hair transplant at the Sikar clinic?
Yes. The Sikar clinic offers hair transplant consultations and procedures. Your doctor first checks your donor area and hair-loss stage, then explains the plan. Read about hair transplant at Kezza.
Do the Sikar doctors also work at the Jaipur clinic?
Yes. Dr. Ankit Bhalothia and Dr. Neelam Choudhary see patients in both Sikar and Jaipur, and both clinics follow the same clinical protocols.
Are PRP and GFC available in Sikar?
Yes. Both PRP and GFC hair treatments are offered at the Sikar clinic. Most GFC courses are 3 to 4 sessions about 4 weeks apart; your doctor advises you after a scalp check. Read about GFC treatment.
Is laser hair removal available in Sikar?
Yes. Laser hair removal is offered at the Sikar clinic. Most people need 6 to 8 sessions, spaced several weeks apart. See laser hair removal.
