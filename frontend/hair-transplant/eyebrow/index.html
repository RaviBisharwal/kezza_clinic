Eyebrow Transplant in Jaipur | Natural, Fuller Brows

An eyebrow transplant rebuilds thin, patchy or over-plucked brows with your own hair. Single-hair grafts, usually taken from the back of the scalp, are placed one by one at the flat angle and direction of natural brow hair, for brows that grow and can be shaped like real eyebrows.

The Procedure

What Is an Eyebrow Transplant?

An eyebrow transplant is a small, precise form of hair transplant. The doctor removes individual follicular units, usually from the back of the scalp, using the FUE technique, and implants them into the brow where hair is thin or missing.

Almost all the grafts are single hairs. Each one is placed at a very flat angle and follows the direction of eyebrow hair: pointing upwards at the inner brow and sideways towards the tail. This detail is what makes a transplanted brow look natural rather than tufted.

Common reasons for an eyebrow transplant include years of over-plucking, waxing or threading, brows that have always been sparse, and gaps from scars, injury or burns. If brow loss is caused by a medical condition, that condition must be treated and under control first.

In Short:

  • Single-hair grafts: placed one by one for a soft, natural look.
  • Natural direction: each hair follows the angle of real brow hair.
  • Real hair: it grows, and can be trimmed and shaped.
Benefits

Why Choose an Eyebrow Transplant?

Real, Growing Hair

Transplanted hair grows like natural hair and can be combed, trimmed and shaped.

Custom Brow Design

The shape, arch and thickness are drawn with you before surgery to suit your face.

Long-Lasting

Grafts come from permanent donor hair, so the result usually lasts for many years.

Day-Care Procedure

Done under local anaesthesia; you go home the same day.

Soft, Natural Look

Single-hair grafts at a flat angle avoid a tufted or planted appearance.

Less Daily Makeup

Many people need far less brow pencil once the new hair has grown in.

Suitability

Who Is a Good Candidate?

The most important points are the cause of your brow loss and a healthy donor area.

An eyebrow transplant may suit you if

  • Your brows are thin or patchy after years of plucking, waxing or threading
  • Your brows have always been sparse, or have gaps from a scar or injury
  • You want fuller brows without relying on pencil, tattoo or microblading
  • You have a healthy donor area and realistic expectations

It may be postponed or not advised if

  • Brow loss is caused by alopecia areata, thyroid disease or another condition that is not yet under control
  • Brow hair is still being lost to ongoing hair-pulling, which needs to be addressed first
  • You have an active skin infection or inflammation around the brows
  • You are not willing to trim the transplanted hairs regularly

If you mainly want colour and shape rather than real hair, microblading or eyebrow PMU may be a quicker option. Our team can explain the difference.

Procedure

How an Eyebrow Transplant Is Done

The procedure usually takes a few hours, depending on the number of grafts.

01

Brow Design

The doctor draws the shape, arch and thickness with you, checking symmetry and how the brows suit your face.

02

Donor Extraction

The donor area, usually at the back of the scalp, is numbed and fine single-hair follicular units are removed with a micro-punch.

03

Graft Selection

Grafts are checked under magnification, and the finest single hairs are chosen to match brow hair.

04

Implantation

After numbing the brows, each graft is placed at a flat angle following the natural direction of brow hair.

05

Aftercare

You receive instructions on cleaning, sleeping position and avoiding rubbing or touching the brows.

Recovery

Eyebrow Transplant Recovery & Results

Brow grafts follow the normal hair growth cycle, so the full result takes months.

D5

Days 1 to 5

Small crusts form around each graft. Mild swelling around the eyes can occur and settles within a few days.

W2

Weeks 1 to 2

The crusts fall away. Avoid rubbing the brows, heavy makeup and direct sun on the area, as advised.

M1

Weeks 2 to 6

Many transplanted hairs shed. This is normal; the roots stay in place.

M4

Months 3 to 4

New brow hairs begin to grow.

M12

Months 6 to 12

The brows fill in, and the final result is usually judged at around a year.

Medical Note: Results vary from person to person. This page is general information from Kezza Hair & Skin Clinic and is not a substitute for a consultation with a doctor.

Designing natural-looking eyebrows

Eyebrows frame the face, so small details matter. The doctor plans three parts of the brow: the head (the inner end, where hairs point upwards), the arch, and the tail (where hairs lie flat and point towards the temple). The design is drawn on your skin and adjusted with you in the mirror until the shape, height and thickness look right for your face.

A slightly conservative design usually ages best. More density can be added in a second session if you want it, but grafts placed too low, too high or in the wrong direction are hard to correct.

Why transplanted brows need trimming

Hair taken from the scalp keeps the growth pattern of scalp hair. Natural eyebrow hair stops at a short length, but transplanted hair keeps growing, so it needs trimming every few weeks. Some people also use a little brow gel or wax to train the hairs to lie flat. Most find this quick and easy once they are used to it.

Eyebrow transplant vs microblading

Eyebrow transplantMicroblading / eyebrow PMU
What it isYour own hair follicles implanted into the browPigment placed in the skin in fine, hair-like strokes
ResultReal hair that grows and can be shapedThe look of fuller brows; no new hair grows
How long it lastsUsually many yearsAbout 12 to 18 months before a touch-up
UpkeepRegular trimmingPeriodic colour touch-ups
When you see the resultGradually, over about a yearStraight away, after healing

Some people start with microblading for an instant result and consider a transplant later for real hair, or combine the two for colour and texture.

Common causes of thin eyebrows

  • Over-plucking, waxing or threading over many years, which can permanently damage follicles.
  • Naturally sparse brows that have been thin since adolescence.
  • Scars from injury, burns or surgery.
  • Medical conditions such as alopecia areata or thyroid disease, which need to be diagnosed and treated first.

If your brows have thinned recently and you do not know why, start with a doctor's examination. A hair loss consultation helps rule out causes that need medical treatment.

Risks and aftercare

Complications are uncommon when an eyebrow transplant is done by trained doctors, and are usually limited to mild pain, swelling and crusting in the first days. Less often, some grafts may not grow, hairs may grow at an unwanted angle or small cysts may form. Keep the area clean, do not rub or scratch the brows, avoid heavy makeup and direct sun while healing, and follow your doctor's instructions.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an eyebrow transplant permanent?
The transplanted follicles come from permanent donor hair, so the result usually lasts for many years. Brow loss caused by a medical condition can return if the condition becomes active again, which is why it must be controlled first.
Do transplanted eyebrows need trimming?
Yes. Because the hair usually comes from the scalp, it keeps growing longer than natural brow hair and needs trimming every few weeks. Some people also use a little brow gel to help the hairs lie flat.
How many grafts are needed for eyebrows?
It depends on whether you are filling small gaps or rebuilding the whole brow. Small corrections need relatively few grafts, while a full brow needs more; the doctor gives you an estimate after drawing the design.
Does an eyebrow transplant hurt?
Both the donor area and the brows are numbed with local anaesthesia, so you feel the injections at the start and little after that. Mild soreness and swelling for a few days are common.
When will I see results from an eyebrow transplant?
New brow hairs usually start to grow around months 3 to 4, after an initial shedding phase. The final result is usually judged at around 12 months.
Is an eyebrow transplant better than microblading?
They do different things. Microblading adds pigment in hair-like strokes; it usually lasts about 12 to 18 months and then needs touch-ups. A transplant grows real hair but is a surgical procedure with a longer wait for results. Some people combine the two.
Can men have an eyebrow transplant?
Yes. The design simply follows a flatter, straighter brow shape and the density that suits a male face.
What if my eyebrows fell out because of a medical condition?
The cause must be diagnosed and controlled first. Conditions such as alopecia areata or thyroid disease are treated medically, and a transplant is considered only when the brow loss has been stable for some time.
