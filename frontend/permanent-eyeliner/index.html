Permanent Eyeliner in Jaipur | Lash Enhancement & Soft Lines

The Technique

What Is Permanent Eyeliner?

Permanent eyeliner is a cosmetic tattoo of the eyelid margin. A fine needle machine places pigment along the lash line, either as tiny dots between the lashes or as a continuous line just above them. Because eyelid skin is very thin and close to the eye, the work is slow, precise and done with the eye protected.

A lash enhancement adds pigment between and at the base of the lashes, so they look thicker and darker without an obvious line. A classic line is a little wider and gives a defined, made-up look. Both are designed around your eye shape, and the thickness can be built up gradually over the first session and the touch-up.

The colour looks darker and thicker for the first few days, then softens as the skin heals. Eyeliner permanent makeup usually lasts two to three years before a refresh, which is longer than eyebrow microblading usually lasts.

In Short:

  • Along the lash line: dots between lashes or a soft line.
  • Precise work: with the eye protected throughout.
  • Lasts 2 to 3 years: with a refresh when it fades.

Suitability

Is Permanent Eyeliner Right for You?

Permanent eyeliner suits most healthy adults, but the eyes need to be healthy on the day.

Permanent eyeliner may suit you if

  • You wear eyeliner or kajal every day and want to save time
  • Your lashes are sparse and you would like them to look fuller
  • Makeup smudges easily on you in heat or humidity
  • You would like defined eyes for sport, travel or a wedding

Wait, or check with us first, if

  • You have an eye infection, a stye, blepharitis, dry eye or other eye problem, or have had eye surgery recently
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding; we recommend waiting until afterwards
  • You have eyelash extensions or use lash-growth serums, which need to be stopped or removed first
  • You form raised scars easily, or have reacted to tattoo pigment or eye makeup before

Contact lenses need to be removed for the appointment; bring your glasses.

Procedure

How a Permanent Eyeliner Appointment Works

Your first appointment includes the consultation, style design and numbing before any pigment.

01

Consultation & Patch Test

At a consultation before your treatment day, the artist checks your eyes and medical history, discusses the style you want and does a patch test so any reaction has time to show.

02

Style Design

The line is mapped to your eye shape and drawn so you can see it. Pigment is applied only after you approve it.

03

Numbing

A numbing cream suitable for the eyelids is applied, and the area is cleaned. Contact lenses are removed.

04

Pigment Application

Pigment is placed along the lash line in small sections while the eye is kept closed and protected.

05

Touch-Up at 4 to 6 Weeks

Once healed, a touch-up fills any gaps and adjusts the thickness.

Healing

Permanent Eyeliner Healing Timeline

Swelling and a darker line are normal at first.

D1

Days 1 to 2

Eyelids are often puffy and the line looks thicker and darker than the final result.

D4

Days 3 to 5

D10

The line may look lighter or uneven while the skin settles.

W6

Weeks 4 to 6

The healed colour shows, and your touch-up session perfects it.

Y2

Two to Three Years

Choosing your eyeliner style

Think about how you wear makeup now. If you mostly want your lashes to look fuller and your eyes defined without an obvious line, a lash enhancement is a good start and can be made bolder later. If you wear liner every day, a soft classic line gives a similar look without the effort. Very thick or dramatic designs are harder to adjust later and fade less evenly, so it is usually better to start slim and build up at the touch-up. Hooded eyelids and the space between lid and brow also affect which style will show when your eyes are open.

Before your appointment

  • Remove eyelash extensions and stop lash-growth serums well before treatment, as advised by your artist.
  • Do not wear eye makeup on the day, and bring your glasses if you use contact lenses.
  • Tell us about eye conditions, recent eye surgery, medicines, and any reactions to eye makeup or pigments.
  • Reschedule if you have a stye, red eye, infection or any irritation on the day.

Aftercare in the first weeks

For the first week, keep the eyelids clean and dry apart from the cleaning routine you are given, apply only the ointment recommended by the clinic, and avoid rubbing your eyes. Skip eye makeup, swimming, saunas and heavy workouts that make you sweat. When you start using eye makeup again, use new products to lower the risk of infection. Contact the clinic straight away if you have increasing redness or discharge, and see an eye doctor urgently for eye pain or any change in your vision.

Risks and how they are reduced

Permanent eyeliner is a cosmetic tattoo close to the eye. The US Food and Drug Administration lists infection, allergic reactions, granulomas and keloids among the risks of tattoos and permanent makeup, and notes that removal is difficult and needs several treatments. Near the eye, pigment spreading beyond the line, loss of some lashes and prolonged swelling are also possible. The risks are reduced by checking your eyes first, sterile single-use needles, careful depth control, protecting the eye throughout, and good aftercare. If you are unhappy with old eyeliner, a PMU correction consultation can explain your options.

What affects the cost of permanent eyeliner?

The cost depends on the style, whether you choose the upper lash line alone, and whether the touch-up is included. Correcting old eyeliner is planned separately. Your artist explains the plan and total at your consultation.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does permanent eyeliner last?
Usually two to three years before it needs a refresh, depending on your skin, the style and your aftercare.
Is permanent eyeliner safe for my eyes?
Complications are uncommon with sterile technique and careful work, but the eyelid is a delicate area. Possible problems include infection, allergic reaction, prolonged swelling, lash loss, small lumps and, very rarely, injury to the eye. Your eyes are protected throughout, and single-use needles are used for every client.
Does permanent eyeliner hurt?
Numbing cream is used, so most people feel a vibrating or scratching sensation. The eyes may water during treatment. The eyelids are usually puffy and slightly tender for a day or two afterwards.
When can I wear contact lenses and eye makeup again?
Remove contact lenses for the appointment and wait until your eyes feel comfortable again before wearing them, usually a few days. Avoid eye makeup, especially mascara, for about a week, and use new products when you start again to lower the risk of infection.
Will my eyes swell?
Some puffiness for one to two days is common. Cool compresses, kept clean and dry, and sleeping with your head slightly raised can help. Contact the clinic if swelling gets worse after the second day or you notice discharge, and for eye pain or any change in your vision, see an eye doctor urgently.
Can I have an MRI after permanent eyeliner?
Yes. The US Food and Drug Administration notes that the risks of avoiding an MRI your doctor has recommended are likely to be much greater than the risks linked to tattoos or permanent makeup. Tell the radiographer about your eyeliner before the scan.
