The Treatment

What Is a Hydra Facial?

A Hydra Facial is a comfortable, 45-minute clinical treatment that deep-cleans congested pores, clears blackheads, and delivers intense hydration with no downtime. It leaves your skin soft, clear, and glowing, making it the perfect monthly reset or pre-event refresher. Unlike parlour facials that rely on painful manual squeezing, it uses gentle vortex suction and medical-grade serums.

How it works: A specialised vortex tip glides smoothly over your skin. As it moves, it creates a gentle fluid motion that loosens dead surface skin and lifts away trapped oil, dirt, and pollution. At the exact same time, the tip infuses calming, hydrating nutrients deep into your newly cleared pores without harsh scrubbing or irritation.

The 4 gentle steps: Every session follows a structured clinical sequence: first, gentle cleansing and exfoliation to remove dull buildup; second, a light pore-softening wash to loosen stubborn blackheads; third, gentle vortex extraction to clear out pores; and fourth, a rich infusion of hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidants to restore moisture.

Everyday skin wellness: In Jaipur's dry, dusty climate, pores easily clog with sweat, dust, and excess sebum. A Hydra Facial gives your skin a complete, refreshing reset. While deeper issues like pitted scars or hormonal melasma require specialized lasers or peels, a Hydra Facial is the gold standard for clean pores and an instant, dewy glow.

In Short:

  • 4-in-1 Care: Deep cleanse, gentle pore loosening, vortex extraction, and intense hydration.
  • No Downtime: Walk out with glowing skin in 45 minutes; return to your routine immediately.
  • Instant Radiance: Skin looks brighter and feels silky-smooth straight after the session.

Suitability

Is a Hydra Facial Right for You?

Hydra Facials are gentle, non-irritating, and suitable for almost all skin types.

A Hydra Facial is ideal if you want to:

  • Clear stubborn blackheads, whiteheads, and oily T-zone congestion
  • Revive dull, tired, or sun-exposed skin with immediate deep moisture
  • Get fresh, radiant, smooth skin before an upcoming wedding, party, or event
  • Maintain clear pores and a healthy skin barrier on a monthly routine

Check with our doctor first, or reschedule if:

  • You have active, inflamed cystic acne, open cuts, or a cold sore flare on the face
  • You are experiencing an acute rash, sunburn, or severe eczema outbreak
  • You recently completed a deep chemical peel or laser session within the last 7 to 10 days
  • You are using strong prescription retinoids or oral isotretinoin without doctor guidance

Have pitted acne scars or deep melasma? A Hydra Facial keeps pores clear, while our doctor-performed acne scar treatment and pigmentation treatment address deep skin layers.

Hydra Facials for Different Skin Types

Because every skin type has unique needs, our doctors adjust the suction intensity and serum formulations during each session:

  • Oily & Congested Skin: The vortex extraction step removes excess sebum and blackheads from the T-zone, helping reduce shine and prevent clogged pores.
  • Dry & Dull Skin: Gentle exfoliation sweeps away rough, flaky dead cells, while concentrated hyaluronic acid deeply quenches parched skin for a soft, plump feel.
  • Sensitive Skin: We use ultra-gentle suction settings and soothing peptide solutions that calm redness and hydrate without irritating delicate barriers.
  • Acne-Prone Skin: Clears trapped oil and dirt from pores to prevent future breakouts; our doctors ensure active inflamed pimples are treated safely.

Understanding the Technology

You may hear terms like hydra facial, hydradermabrasion, or aqua facial. HydraFacial is the trademarked brand of one specific manufacturer, while hydradermabrasion is the medical science behind fluid-based vortex exfoliation. At Kezza Clinic, we utilize advanced medical hydradermabrasion technology that follows the same proven clinical stages: deep fluid cleansing, gentle pore softening, vacuum extraction, and antioxidant serum infusion. What matters most for your skin is hygienic medical standards, single-use tips, and doctor-calibrated settings tailored to your skin type.

Simple Aftercare for Lasting Glow

  • Keep It Hydrated: Drink plenty of water and use a gentle, fragrance-free moisturiser.
  • Protect From Sun: Apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ sunscreen daily, especially when heading outdoors in Rajasthan.
  • Take a 48-Hour Break from Actives: Avoid facial scrubs, bleaching creams, waxing, chemical peels, and retinol for 2 days after treatment.
  • Let Skin Breathe: Avoid heavy foundation or intense gym workouts for the rest of the day to keep fresh pores clean.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a hydra facial cost in Jaipur?
At Kezza Clinic, a hydra facial costs ₹5,000 per session, and most people have one session a month. It is performed under Dr. Neelam Choudhary and Dr. Amrita Mukhija at our Jaipur and Sikar clinics.
What is the difference between a Hydra Facial and a parlour facial?
Parlour facials often rely on harsh manual squeezing with metal pins or fingernails, which can bruise tissue and damage capillaries. A medical Hydra Facial at Kezza uses gentle vortex suction and medical-grade single-use tips. It cleans pores deeply and infuses medical-grade active ingredients like hyaluronic acid without tissue marks and protecting against bacterial contamination.
Does a Hydra Facial hurt or cause peeling?
No. The procedure is very comfortable and relaxing, feeling like a light, cool massage on the face. There is no harsh peeling, no scabbing, and no downtime. You can return to work or social plans immediately.
How often should I get a Hydra Facial?
For general skin maintenance, a session about once a month is usually recommended. This fits your skin's natural renewal cycle and helps keep pores from clogging with dust and oil.
When should I get a Hydra Facial before a wedding or party?
We recommend scheduling your appointment 2 to 4 days before your function. This gives the skin time to settle into peak luminosity, giving you a smooth, glowing canvas for photography and makeup.
Can a Hydra Facial help with acne and blackheads?
Yes. It is particularly effective for blackheads, whiteheads, and congested, oily skin. However, if you have severe, painful cystic acne, our doctors will first treat the active inflammation with targeted medical therapy before recommending suction treatments.
What aftercare should I follow after my facial?
Aftercare is straightforward: keep your skin hydrated, drink plenty of water, and apply broad-spectrum sunscreen daily. Avoid heavy workouts, steam rooms, and direct sun for 24 hours, and skip harsh facial scrubs, bleaching creams, or retinol for 48 hours.
How long does a session take?
A complete Hydra Facial session takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes, including skin evaluation, preparation, treatment stages, and final sun protection.
Is Hydra Facial suitable for sensitive Indian skin?
Yes. Because our doctors customize the suction level and the concentration of active serums for your specific skin type, the Hydra Facial is safe, gentle, and effective for all Indian skin tones.
