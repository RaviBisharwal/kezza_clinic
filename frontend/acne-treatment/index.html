Targeted Medical Approach

Targeted 4-Step Scar & Acne Solutions

Different types of acne scars need different medical treatments. Our dermatologists combine proven clinical techniques to smooth rough texture, lift deep depressions, and stop future breakouts.

Step 01 — Scar Lifting

Subcision (Deep Scar Release)

A fine, specialized medical tool gently releases the tight fibrous bands under the skin that pull scars down. This allows depressed rolling scars to lift back up naturally.

Best for: Rolling & Deep Pitted Scars
Step 02 — Collagen Rebuilding

Secret RF Microneedling

Ultra-fine gold-tipped micro-needles deliver gentle radiofrequency energy deep into the skin, stimulating powerful new collagen production to fill and smooth pitted scars.

Best for: Boxcar Scars & Open Pores
Step 03 — Surface Smoothing

Fractional CO2 Laser

Micro-laser beams gently polish rough, uneven surface layers of skin, encouraging fresh, healthy skin cell growth and smoothing out stubborn pitted texture.

Best for: Uneven Texture & Deep Pits
Active Breakout Control

Medical Clarifying Peels

Doctor-formulated chemical peels clear deep trapped oil, kill acne bacteria, regulate excess oiliness, and rapidly fade post-acne dark spots.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert Guidance for Acne & Scars

Clear, direct answers provided by the senior dermatology team at Kezza Clinic.

How many sessions of Subcision & MNRF are required for acne scars?
Most patients see noticeable smoothing within 3 to 5 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. Deep tethered scars begin lifting after the first 2 sessions, with skin texture continuing to improve as new collagen forms over several months.
Is subcision treatment painful and what is the recovery time?
Not at all. We apply a soothing medical numbing cream before the procedure so you remain comfortable throughout. Mild redness or swelling settles within 2 to 3 days, and you can return to normal work and daily activities within 24 to 48 hours.
Can active acne and acne scars be treated together?
Yes, absolutely. Our dermatologists use a phased approach: we first calm active, painful pimples with gentle clarifying peels and medications, and then introduce scar-lifting lasers once active breakouts are under control.
Are fractional laser and RF microneedling safe for Indian and darker skin types (Fitzpatrick IV–VI)?
Yes, absolutely. Our advanced RF microneedling uses insulated gold-tipped micro-needles specifically calibrated for Indian skin tones. The energy works safely beneath the surface layer, preventing post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (dark marks or burns).
