What is Permanent Makeup?

Permanent makeup, also known as cosmetic tattooing or PMU, is a semi-permanent beauty enhancement where specialized pigments are implanted into the upper layers of your skin using fine needles. Unlike traditional tattoos, PMU uses softer, more natural pigments that fade gradually — typically lasting 1–3 years before a touch-up is needed.

At Kezza Clinic, our PMU artists are specially trained to work with Indian skin tones, selecting pigments that perfectly complement your natural complexion and undertones — so your results always look soft, natural, and uniquely yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about permanent makeup at Kezza Clinic, Jaipur

Is permanent makeup painful? +

Most clients describe the sensation as mild discomfort rather than pain. We apply a topical numbing cream before the procedure, which significantly reduces any discomfort. The sensation is often compared to light scratching or tweezing. Some areas, like the lips, may be slightly more sensitive, but our numbing techniques ensure you remain comfortable throughout the procedure.

How long does permanent makeup last? +

Permanent makeup typically lasts 1-3 years, depending on several factors including your skin type, lifestyle, sun exposure, and the area treated. Eyebrow microblading usually lasts 12-18 months, while permanent eyeliner and lip blush can last 2-3 years. Regular touch-ups help maintain the color and shape, ensuring your permanent makeup always looks fresh and natural.

Is permanent makeup safe for all skin types? +

Yes, permanent makeup is safe for most skin types when performed by a trained professional using quality pigments and proper techniques. However, certain conditions like active skin infections, severe allergies, or certain medical conditions may require special consideration. During your consultation at Kezza Clinic, we'll assess your skin and medical history to ensure the treatment is safe for you.

Is permanent makeup suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding? +

We recommend waiting until after pregnancy and breastfeeding before getting permanent makeup. While the procedure is generally safe, we prefer to err on the side of caution during this special time. Once you've finished breastfeeding, you can safely proceed with permanent makeup treatments at Kezza Clinic.

Can I do permanent makeup if I already have old PMU work? +

Yes! At Kezza Clinic, we specialize in correcting and refreshing old permanent makeup. During your consultation, we'll assess your existing work and discuss options for correction, color adjustment, or complete rework. Our artists are skilled in working over existing PMU to create beautiful, natural-looking results.

How far in advance should brides book their permanent makeup? +

We recommend booking your permanent makeup consultation at least 2-3 months before your wedding date. This allows time for the initial procedure, healing period (7-14 days), and a touch-up session 4-6 weeks later. This ensures your permanent makeup is fully healed and looks perfect on your special day. Early booking is especially important during peak wedding seasons in Jaipur.

What should I expect during the healing process? +

The healing process typically takes 7-14 days. During the first few days, you may experience mild redness, slight swelling, and the color may appear darker than expected. Around days 4-7, you may notice some flaking or scabbing – this is normal. The color will soften and lighten as it heals. After 2 weeks, the true color will emerge, and it will continue to settle over the next few weeks. A touch-up session after 4-6 weeks perfects the final result.

Can I choose the color and style of my permanent makeup? +

Absolutely! During your consultation at Kezza Clinic, we'll discuss your preferences, show you color options, and create a custom design that complements your features and skin tone. Our artists work with you to ensure the color and style match your vision while ensuring it looks natural and suits your face shape. We'll test colors on your skin before the procedure to ensure you're happy with the choice.
