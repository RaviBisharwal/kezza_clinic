Frequently Asked Questions

All laser hair removal devices — diode, Nd:YAG, alexandrite, IPL — work by targeting melanin pigment in the hair shaft. The laser energy is absorbed by melanin, converted to heat, and conducted to the follicular root to destroy it. White, grey, and blonde hair contains no melanin at all. The laser beam passes straight through without generating any heat — making zero permanent reduction possible, regardless of the machine, wavelength, or number of sessions.

Yes. When the electrothermal micro-probe delivers sufficient energy directly to the follicular germinal matrix (the "growth center" of each hair), it is permanently deactivated and cannot regenerate. The FDA recognizes electrolysis as the only proven permanent hair removal method. Multiple sessions are required because only hairs in the active (anagen) growth phase are fully susceptible in each session — a natural biological requirement, not a technology limitation.

Most patients require 6 to 12 sessions spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart. The exact number depends on the treatment area size, hair density, follicular depth, and individual hair growth cycles. Smaller zones like the upper lip may clear in 4 to 6 sessions while large areas like the full beard or back require 8 to 14 sessions. Your digital trichoscopy mapping session creates a precise personalized clearance plan.

We apply a strong pharmaceutical-grade topical anesthetic cream 45 minutes before each session, combined with a vibration cooling device during treatment. Mild redness or sensitivity settles within 4 to 12 hours.
