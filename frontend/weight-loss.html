✦ FAQs

Weight Management & Body Sculpting: Common Questions

Straight answers from our doctors. Available at our Jaipur and Sikar clinics.

What is cryolipolysis (fat freezing)?

Cryolipolysis is a non-surgical treatment that cools a targeted pocket of fat under controlled conditions. Some of the chilled fat cells are damaged and your body clears them naturally over the following weeks. At Kezza it is used for stubborn areas such as the belly, love handles, thighs and under the chin.

Is non-surgical body sculpting a weight-loss treatment?

Not on its own. Cryolipolysis and HIFU body sculpting reduce and reshape stubborn fat pockets and tighten skin, so you lose inches rather than kilos. They work best for people close to a healthy weight. For overall weight loss, our doctors pair them with a supervised diet and lifestyle plan. Learn the difference between body sculpting and weight loss.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the area and how much fat there is. Many people need one to three sessions per area, spaced several weeks apart. Your doctor sets the number at your body-composition assessment, after checking your measurements and goals.

When will I see results?

Results build gradually. Changes can start to show about three weeks after a cryolipolysis session, with clearer improvement after about two months and the full effect of a session typically seen at around three to four months, as your body clears the treated fat cells.

Is there any downtime, and what does it feel like?

There is no hospital stay and most people return to normal activities the same day. During cryolipolysis you feel strong cold and a pulling sensation that eases as the area numbs. Temporary redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness or numbness can last from a few days to a few weeks.

Who should not have cryolipolysis?

It is not suitable during pregnancy, for people with cold-sensitivity conditions such as cryoglobulinaemia, cold urticaria or Raynaud's phenomenon, or for obesity treatment. Your doctor reviews your medical history before recommending it, and will explain rare side effects such as paradoxical fat enlargement in the treated area.
