About Dr. Mandhata Sharma

Dr. Mandhata Sharma is an ENT and head and neck surgeon who completed his MS in Otorhinolaryngology at Maulana Azad Medical College, University of Delhi.

He has specialised experience in rhinoplasty and endoscopic ENT procedures, with treatment planned around both how the nose looks and how it works.

Because he is an ENT surgeon, his rhinoplasty consultations include the inside of the nose — the septum and airway — so that problems such as a blocked nose can be corrected in the same operation (septorhinoplasty). He visits our Jaipur clinic as a consultant and performs rhinoplasty; our other rhinoplasty surgeon is plastic surgeon Dr. Nakul Somani.
