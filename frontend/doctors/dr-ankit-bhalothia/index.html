About Dr. Ankit Bhalothia

Dr. Ankit Bhalothia is a specialist oral and maxillofacial surgeon whose practice at Kezza approaches facial aesthetics and hair transplantation through a lens of structural harmony. He treats the face as a whole, with an understanding of how the underlying bone structure shapes what we see on the surface.

While trained in complex oral and facial surgery, his main clinical focus is modern hair restoration and facial rejuvenation. He specialises in designing hairlines that frame each person's facial proportions, using micro-follicular techniques — FUE and DHI — that handle grafts with care.

At Kezza he also performs blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery) and scar revision, and supervises hair-regrowth treatments such as PRP and GFC for people who are not ready for, or do not need, a transplant.
