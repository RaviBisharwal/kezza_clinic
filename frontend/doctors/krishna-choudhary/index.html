About Krishna Choudhary

Krishna is a skilled and passionate permanent makeup artist with extensive hands-on experience in PMU and aesthetic enhancement procedures.

She specialises in natural-looking results tailored to each individual's features — from hair-stroke microblading to lip neutralization for dark lips and soft lip tints.

Known for meticulous attention to detail and a patient-centred approach, she makes comfort, safety and hygiene part of every appointment. If you have a medical question — such as cold sores, a skin condition or a medicine you take — one of our clinic doctors can advise before your appointment.
