Profile

About Dr. Harshit Sharma

Dr. Harshit Sharma is a specialist oral and maxillofacial surgeon (BDS 2021, MDS 2026, ATLS-ACS 2025) whose practice at Kezza approaches facial aesthetics, structural reconstruction, and hair transplantation through a lens of total anatomical harmony. Trained in complex maxillofacial surgical techniques, he treats facial form and soft-tissue balance as a unified craft.

His foremost clinical specialty is hair transplant consultation and precision surgery, designing bespoke, natural hairlines that frame each patient's facial proportions using advanced micro-follicular FUE and DHI techniques.

In facial aesthetics and maxillofacial care, Dr. Harshit specializes in lobuloplasty (earlobe repair and reshaping), blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery), Botox & dermal fillers, facial scar revision, facial trauma and reconstruction, thread lift procedures, as well as oral pathology evaluations and biopsies. He also supervises hair-regrowth treatments including PRP and GFC.
