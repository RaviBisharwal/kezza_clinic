About Dr. Aliza Rizvi

Dr. Aliza Rizvi's surgical foundation brings a deep command of facial anatomy, bone structure and nerve pathways to every procedure.

Leading our Ajmer centre, she applies rigorous, hospital-grade surgical discipline to non-surgical facial rejuvenation and to delicate micro-graft hair transplants, aiming for dense, natural-looking results.

Patients in Ajmer see her for hair transplant, hair-loss diagnosis and facial aesthetics, alongside Dr. Dhiral Vijayvargiya.
