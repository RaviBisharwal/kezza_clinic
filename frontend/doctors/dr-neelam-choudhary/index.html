About Dr. Neelam Choudhary

Dr. Neelam Choudhary is a dental surgeon with specialized postgraduate training in facial aesthetics and clinical cosmetology.

Her clinical work includes skin rejuvenation procedures, laser therapies, chemical and medical peels, microneedling and tailored cosmetic treatment plans built on scientifically backed protocols.

At Kezza she performs Q-switch laser treatment for pigmentation and ink. She also sees patients for chemical and medical peels, microneedling, full body laser hair removal, hydra facials and glutathione, and shares the medical weight-loss programme with the Kezza clinical team.
