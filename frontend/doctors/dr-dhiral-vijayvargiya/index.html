About Dr. Dhiral Vijayvargiya

Dr. Dhiral Vijayvargiya's surgical foundation in oral and maxillofacial surgery brings a detailed understanding of facial anatomy, bone structure and nerve pathways.

Dr. Dhiral applies that hospital-grade surgical discipline to delicate micro-graft hair transplants and to non-surgical facial rejuvenation, with the aim of dense, natural-looking restorations that suit the face.

At Kezza Ajmer, Dr. Dhiral sees patients for hair transplant planning, hair-loss diagnosis and facial aesthetic treatments, working alongside Dr. Aliza Rizvi.
