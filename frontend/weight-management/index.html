Medical Weight Management in Jaipur | Doctor-Supervised Plans

Medical weight management is a doctor-supervised plan to lose weight safely and keep it off, built around your health, habits and goals rather than a crash diet. The Approach

Why Take a Medical Approach to Weight?

Body weight is shaped by much more than willpower: what and when you eat, how active you are, sleep, stress, medicines, hormones and family history all play a part. Crash diets often lead to quick losses that return. A supervised plan looks at the whole picture, sets a steady pace and adjusts as you go.

For Asian Indians, health risks start at a lower weight than in many other populations, because more body fat tends to be carried around the waist. The India Obesity Commission's 2025 definition uses a body mass index (BMI) above 23 together with waist measurement and health effects, rather than weight alone. That is why your waist and your health are checked, not just the number on the scale.

Small changes matter. The NHS notes that losing just 5 to 10 percent of your body weight can improve your health, and it recommends aiming for about 0.5 to 1 kg a week. A steady plan you can live with is more useful than a fast one you cannot keep up.

In Short:

  • Health first: your assessment looks beyond the scale.
  • Steady pace: about half to one kilo a week.
  • Small losses count: 5 to 10 percent can improve health.
Your Plan

What Your Plan Can Include

Health Assessment

Weight, BMI, waist measurement and medical history. Your doctor may suggest tests, for example for blood sugar, thyroid or cholesterol.

Eating Plan

Realistic changes that fit home-cooked Indian food, your work hours and your family routine, not a one-size diet chart.

Activity Plan

A gradual build-up of walking, strength and other activity that suits your fitness, joints and schedule.

Sleep & Stress

Practical steps for sleep and stress, which affect appetite and cravings.

Regular Follow-Up

Check-ins to track weight and waist, solve problems and adjust the plan so you stay on course.

Contouring Later

Once you are close to your goal, cryolipolysis or HIFU can be considered for areas that stay stubborn.

Suitability

Who Is Medical Weight Management For?

It suits adults who want structured, medical support to lose weight and keep it off.

A consultation may help if

  • Your BMI or waist measurement is above the healthy range
  • You have tried diets before and the weight came back
  • You have a health condition linked to weight and want a plan that takes it into account, alongside your own doctor
  • You want regular support and follow-up rather than managing alone

Tell your doctor if

  • You are pregnant, planning a pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • You have diabetes, heart disease, thyroid disease or another long-term condition, or take regular medicines, so the plan can be made safe for you
  • You have had an eating disorder, in which case specialist support comes first
  • You are under 18; weight management for children and teenagers is handled differently

Body contouring treatments such as cryolipolysis and HIFU body sculpting reshape specific areas. They are not a substitute for weight loss.

BMI and waist targets for Indians

The World Health Organization defines overweight as a BMI of 25 or more and obesity as 30 or more, but these cut-offs were set mainly from Western populations. For Asian Indians, experts use lower numbers, because health problems such as diabetes appear at a lower BMI. The India Obesity Commission's revised definition, published in 2025, uses a BMI above 23 as the starting point and adds waist measurement or waist-to-height ratio and the effect of weight on health and daily life. Your doctor uses these measures to understand your risk and to set targets, not to label you.

Eating well without a crash diet

You do not need special foods to lose weight. Most plans work by gently reducing total calories, for example with smaller portions of rice and rotis, more vegetables and dal, protein at each meal, fewer fried snacks and sweets, and fewer sugary drinks. The NHS suggests cutting down on high-fat and sugary foods and eating plenty of fruit and vegetables. Regular meal times and planning for festivals, travel and eating out help you stay on track without feeling deprived.

Building activity into your day

Activity supports weight loss and, even more, helps you keep weight off. The NHS suggests building up to 45 to 60 minutes of activity a day when you are losing weight, which can be split into shorter sessions. Brisk walking is a good start for most people; strength exercises two or more days a week help protect muscle. Your plan starts where you are and builds up gradually, taking into account any joint pain or health conditions.

Where body contouring fits

Some areas, such as the lower belly or love handles, can stay stubborn even after successful weight loss. Once you are close to your goal and your weight is steady, non-surgical treatments such as cryolipolysis or HIFU body sculpting can reduce these pockets. They are planned after weight loss, not instead of it, because they change shape rather than weight.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the medical weight-loss programme cost?
At Kezza Clinic, the medical weight-loss programme costs ₹5,000 per month and includes 4 sessions in that month. Any tests or prescription medicines are discussed, with their cost, before you start.
How much weight can I safely lose?
The NHS suggests aiming for about 0.5 to 1 kg a week. Faster loss is harder to keep off and can mean losing muscle as well as fat. Your doctor sets a target that suits your health.
Do you prescribe weight-loss medicines?
Yes, when a medicine is suitable for you. In line with Indian rules, weight-loss medicines are prescribed only by our internal medicine specialist at our Jaipur clinic, after a consultation, for some adults with obesity, or with overweight and a weight-related health problem. They are always used together with changes to eating and activity and regular follow-up, and your doctor explains the likely benefits and side effects first.
Is this a diet programme?
No. There are no crash diets or meal replacements by default. The plan works with the food you already eat, with changes to portions, timing and choices that you can keep up with your family.
Why does my waist size matter?
Fat stored around the waist is linked to a higher risk of diabetes and heart disease. Guidance for Asian Indians looks at waist measurement as well as BMI, because many people carry more abdominal fat even at a normal weight.
Can cryolipolysis or HIFU help me lose weight?
They reduce fat in a small area and change your shape, but they do not bring down your overall weight. They work best once you are close to your goal, for areas that stay stubborn.
Do I need blood tests?
Not always. Your doctor may suggest tests, for example for blood sugar, thyroid function or cholesterol, if your history or measurements point to a problem that could affect your weight or your plan.
