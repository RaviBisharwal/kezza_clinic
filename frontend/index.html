Common Questions

The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia, so you stay awake but feel no pain during it. Most patients describe mild soreness for two to three days afterwards, which is managed with simple prescribed medication.

A typical session runs four to eight hours in a single day, depending on the number of grafts. You walk out the same day — no hospital stay is required.

Transplanted follicles are taken from the donor area at the back of the scalp, which is genetically resistant to hair loss. Those grafts are permanent. Existing non-transplanted hair can still thin over time, which is why we plan the hairline with your long-term pattern in mind.

Cost depends on the graft count and technique, so it is confirmed only after a scalp assessment. We offer no-cost EMI plans on most procedures. Book a free consultation and you will get a written estimate before committing to anything.

Most patients resume desk work in three to five days. Visible scabbing settles within about ten days, and new growth typically becomes noticeable from the third to fourth month, with full results at twelve months.

Adults with stable hair loss and a healthy donor area are usually suitable. Age, the pattern and rate of loss, and general health all matter, so candidacy is confirmed by our surgeon during the consultation rather than online.
