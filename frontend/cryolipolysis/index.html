Cryolipolysis in Jaipur | Non-Surgical Fat Freezing

Cryolipolysis, often called fat freezing, reduces a stubborn pocket of fat by cooling it under carefully controlled conditions, with no surgery or injections. It is designed to reshape specific areas, not to bring down your weight. At Kezza, our doctors plan cryolipolysis after a body-composition assessment at our Jaipur and Sikar clinics.

How It Works

How Does Fat Freezing Work?

Fat cells are more sensitive to cold than the skin, nerves and muscle around them. During cryolipolysis, an applicator draws a fold of fat into a cup with gentle suction, or rests flat against the skin, and cools that area to a set temperature for a fixed time. The cold injures many of the fat cells in the pocket while the skin is protected by a gel pad.

The injured fat cells are not removed on the day. Over the following weeks, they break down and the body's immune system clears them away naturally, so the layer of fat in the treated area gradually becomes thinner. That is why the result is judged after three to four months rather than when you walk out of the clinic.

The fat cells that are cleared do not grow back. The fat cells that remain in your body can still get bigger if you gain weight, though, both in the treated area and elsewhere, so keeping a steady weight is what protects the new shape.

In Short:

  • Spot reduction: for pinchable pockets of fat.
  • Gradual change: results build over two to four months.
  • No surgery: most people return to their day straight after.
Treatment Areas

Areas Commonly Treated With Cryolipolysis

Belly

The upper and lower abdomen, one of the most requested areas, often treated with more than one applicator.

Love Handles

Fat on the flanks, just above the hips, which often stays even when you eat well and exercise.

Thighs

Inner and outer thighs, treated with applicators shaped for each area.

Under the Chin

A smaller applicator treats fat under the chin, where there is enough fat to draw into the cup.

Arms & Back

Upper arms, bra-line fat and the lower back, depending on how much fat can be drawn into the applicator.

Several Areas at Once

Kezza's full-body option uses four applicators, so more than one area can be treated in the same visit.

Suitability

Who Is Cryolipolysis Suitable For?

Cryolipolysis reshapes rather than slims. It works best for people who are close to a healthy weight and have one or two areas that will not shift.

Cryolipolysis may suit you if

  • You are at or close to a healthy weight, with a pinchable pocket of fat
  • Diet and exercise have not shifted a particular area, such as the belly or love handles
  • You would prefer a non-surgical option and can wait a few months for the full result
  • You are ready to keep your weight steady after treatment

Cryolipolysis is not suitable if

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have a cold-related condition such as cryoglobulinaemia, cold agglutinin disease, paroxysmal cold haemoglobinuria, cold urticaria or Raynaud's phenomenon
  • You have nerve damage or reduced feeling in the area, for example from diabetes, or eczema, psoriasis or dermatitis where the applicator would sit
  • You have a hernia in or near the area to be treated
  • Your main goal is to lose a lot of weight; cryolipolysis is not a treatment for obesity

If overall weight loss is your first goal, start with our medical weight management programme, and consider cryolipolysis later for the areas that remain.

Procedure

What Happens During a Cryolipolysis Session

Each applicator stays on for up to about an hour. You can rest, read or use your phone during treatment.

01

Body-Composition Assessment

Your doctor reviews your medical history, measures the area and checks that cryolipolysis, rather than another option, suits your goal.

02

Marking & Photos

The area is marked, measured and photographed so that changes can be compared at your review.

03

Cooling

A protective gel pad is placed on the skin and the applicator is fitted. You feel pulling and strong cold for the first few minutes, and then the area goes numb.

04

Massage

When the applicator comes off, the area may be massaged for a few minutes.

05

Back to Your Day

Most people go back to normal activities straight away. A review is booked to compare photos and measurements.

Cryolipolysis or liposuction?

Liposuction is surgery. Fat is removed through small cuts under anaesthesia, and a larger amount can be taken in one procedure, but there is a recovery period and the usual risks of surgery. Cryolipolysis has no cuts or anaesthesia and most people carry on with their day afterwards, but the change per session is modest and appears gradually. It is a good fit for a small, well-defined bulge in someone close to a healthy weight. If you have a larger amount of fat to lose, or loose skin, your doctor will tell you honestly that another approach is likely to suit you better.

Full-body sessions with four applicators

Kezza's full-body cryo session uses four applicators at the same time, so that several zones, for example both flanks and the lower belly, can be treated in one visit instead of across several appointments. Each area still has to be suitable on its own, with enough pinchable fat for the applicator and no medical reason to avoid cooling. Your doctor decides which areas to include and how the applicators are placed, and you should expect the visit to take longer than a single-area session.

How to prepare for your session

  • Tell your doctor about any medical conditions, especially cold-related conditions, nerve problems, hernias or recent surgery near the area.
  • Mention any medicines you take, and if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing that is easy to move away from the area being treated.
  • Aim to be at a stable weight before you start, so that the change from treatment is easier to see.

Aftercare and getting the best result

There are no strict activity restrictions after cryolipolysis, and most people return to work the same day. Soreness, numbness and swelling settle on their own; ask your doctor what pain relief is safe for you if the area is uncomfortable. Keep up regular activity and balanced meals while your body clears the treated fat, because gaining weight during this time can hide the change. Your review appointment, usually after three to four months, compares measurements and photos with your first visit.

Side effects to know about

Most side effects are temporary: redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness, itching, tingling and numbness in the treated area, lasting from a few days to a few weeks. Paradoxical adipose hyperplasia (PAH) is rare. In PAH, weeks to months after treatment, the treated area becomes larger and firmer instead of smaller. The American Society of Plastic Surgeons reports that it affects fewer than 1 percent of patients and is more common in men. It does not resolve on its own, and treatment usually involves liposuction. If a treated area starts to grow after first looking smaller, contact the clinic. Rarely, the cold can injure the skin (blistering or frostbite), and some people develop strong nerve pain a few days after treatment; contact the clinic straight away if either happens.

What affects the cost of cryolipolysis?

The cost depends on how many areas are treated, how many applicators each area needs and whether you choose a second session. Treating one small area costs less than a full-body session with four applicators. Your doctor explains the plan and the number of sessions after your assessment, before you decide.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is cryolipolysis a weight-loss treatment?
No. It reduces the fat in a treated area, so the change shows in your measurements and how clothes fit rather than on the weighing scale. It suits people who are already close to a healthy weight. For overall weight loss, a supervised plan for diet, activity and health comes first.
How much fat does cryolipolysis remove?
Results vary from person to person. Cleveland Clinic reports that studies show an average fat reduction of between 15 and 28 percent in the treated area at around four months, and the American Society of Plastic Surgeons gives an average of about 20 percent. Your doctor will explain what is realistic after measuring your area.
Does fat freezing hurt?
You feel strong cold, pulling and some aching for the first few minutes, until the area goes numb. Afterwards the area can feel tender, tingly, itchy or numb for a few days to a few weeks. The American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes the soreness as similar to having worked out.
How many cryolipolysis sessions will I need?
Some people are happy after one session per area, while others choose a second session for a further reduction. Cleveland Clinic notes that repeat sessions are usually spaced six to eight weeks apart. Your doctor recommends a plan at your assessment.
Is the fat gone for good?
The fat cells that are destroyed are removed by the body and do not come back. If you gain weight later, the remaining fat cells can grow, in the treated area and elsewhere, so a steady weight and healthy routine are what maintain the result.
What are the side effects and risks?
Common effects are redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness, tingling and numbness, which settle over days to weeks. A rare side effect called paradoxical adipose hyperplasia makes the treated fat grow larger instead of smaller. It does not go away on its own and may need surgery to correct, so your doctor will discuss it before you decide.
