FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clinical Hair Loss FAQs

Clear scientific answers regarding expectations, timelines, and procedural safety.

At what stage of hair loss should I begin clinical treatment?
The ideal time to begin treatment is at the very first signs of noticeable shedding or miniaturization (Norwood Stage 1-2). Early intervention preserves existing hair follicles before the roots undergo permanent fibrosis, preventing the need for surgical hair restoration later in life.
What is the difference between PRP and GFC therapy?
While both therapies utilize your own blood, GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) is an advanced next-generation therapy where platelets are completely activated and cell debris is removed. This yields a much higher concentration of pure growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, EGF, IGF-1) with minimal discomfort, swelling, or burning during injections.
How soon can I expect visible hair regrowth?
Noticeable stabilization of hair fall typically begins within 3 to 4 weeks after your first session. Visible follicular thickening, increased hair density, and new baby hair emergence generally become apparent between months 3 and 4 as follicles transition from telogen back into the active anagen growth phase.
Is clinical hair loss treatment painful?
No. At Kezza Clinic, patient comfort is our utmost priority. We utilize medical-grade topical numbing creams alongside targeted vibration anesthesia and ultra-fine micro-needles (31G-32G). The vast majority of our patients experience minimal to no discomfort throughout their procedure.
Are the results of hair restoration permanent?
Surgical hair transplants (Bio-FUE) utilize DHT-resistant donor follicles from the permanent donor zone, ensuring that transplanted follicles grow for a lifetime. For non-surgical treatments like PRP and GFC, native miniaturized hair is stimulated and preserved; periodic maintenance sessions (once every 4 to 6 months) sustain these results indefinitely.
