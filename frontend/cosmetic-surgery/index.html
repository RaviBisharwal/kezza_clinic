How It Works

How cosmetic surgery works at Kezza

The same careful steps for every procedure.

  1. 01Consultation

    Meet your surgeon

    You discuss what bothers you and what you hope for. The surgeon examines you and explains what is realistic — including when surgery is not the right answer.

  2. 02Plan

    Written estimate

    You receive a plan with the procedure, anaesthesia, recovery time and cost, and time to think it over.

  3. 03Preparation

    Tests and fitness

    Routine tests and a fitness check are arranged when needed, with advice on smoking, medicines and supplements.

  4. 04Procedure day

    Day care

    Most facial procedures are day-care operations under local or general anaesthesia.

  5. 05Recovery

    Aftercare

    Clear written aftercare, a contact number for questions, and stitches or splints removed at follow-up.

  6. 06Reviews

    Until it settles

    Review visits continue while swelling settles and scars mature over the following months.

Before You Decide

Choosing cosmetic surgery safely

Cosmetic surgery is a personal decision, and a good result depends as much on the planning as on the operation. Before any procedure, it is worth checking a few things:

  • Who will operate. Ask about the surgeon's training and experience with your procedure. At Kezza you meet the surgeon at your consultation.
  • What is realistic. Photographs and a frank discussion help you understand what can change — and what cannot.
  • The full cost. Ask what the quote includes: anaesthesia, medicines, dressings, follow-up visits and what happens if a touch-up is needed.
  • Recovery. Plan time off work and help at home, and avoid booking surgery just before a big event.
  • Your reasons. Surgery works best when it is something you want for yourself. If worries about your appearance take up a lot of your day, talk them through first.

If a non-surgical treatment would suit you better — for example Botox or fillers for lines, or microneedling for acne scars — your doctor will tell you.
