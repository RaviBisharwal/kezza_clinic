Advanced Regenerative Medicine

GFC Therapy for Hair Regrowth

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) represents the latest clinical evolution beyond conventional PRP therapy.

What Makes GFC Highly Effective?

Unlike standard PRP which injects whole platelets along with white and red blood cells, GFC utilizes an engineered acellular extract. The patient's blood is collected in specialized activation tubes that trigger the platelets to release their stored growth factors directly into the serum.

Key biologically active components in Kezza GFC include:

  • PDGF (Platelet-Derived Growth Factor): Stimulates cellular proliferation and vascular angiogenesis around follicle bulbs.
  • VEGF (Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor): Boosts micro-capillary circulation around the dermal papilla.
  • EGF (Epidermal Growth Factor): Supports follicular epithelial cell mitosis and active anagen phase elongation.
  • IGF-1 (Insulin-like Growth Factor 1): Prevents premature entry into catagen hair shedding phase.

Key Clinical Advantages of GFC

Acellular Formulation:

Zero RBCs or WBCs means virtually no injection stinging, inflammatory tenderness, or post-session swelling.

Consistent Potency:

Delivers higher, standardized concentrations of critical growth factors per milliliter of injected concentrate.

Fewer Clinic Visits:

A typical clinical course requires only 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 weeks apart, compared to 6 to 8 sessions of PRP.

Transplant Synergism:

Excellent pre- and post-transplant therapy to support native hair and accelerate transplanted graft take.
