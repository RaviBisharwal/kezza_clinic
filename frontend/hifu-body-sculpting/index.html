HIFU Body Sculpting in Jaipur | Focused Ultrasound

HIFU body sculpting uses high-intensity focused ultrasound to heat and break down fat cells in a chosen area, such as the belly or love handles, without cuts or needles. The body then clears the treated fat over the following weeks. At Kezza, HIFU body sculpting is planned by our doctors at our Jaipur and Sikar clinics.

How It Works

How Does HIFU Body Sculpting Work?

Ultrasound waves can pass through the skin harmlessly. When they are focused to a small point at a set depth, they create intense heat just at that point, in the same way a magnifying glass concentrates sunlight. In HIFU body sculpting, the focal point is placed in the layer of fat under the skin.

A review in Research and Reports in Focused Ultrasound explains that the focused energy heats the fat to about 60 to 65 °C, causing the fat cells at that depth to break down, while the skin surface is not damaged. Over the following weeks, the body's clean-up cells remove the treated fat, so the fat layer becomes thinner gradually.

HIFU is also used on the face for skin lifting, with different devices and depths. Body sculpting is aimed at fat reduction in a defined area. Like cryolipolysis, it reshapes; it is not a way to lose weight.

In Short:

  • Heat, not cold: focused ultrasound breaks down fat cells.
  • One or more sessions: depending on the device and area.
  • Gradual result: judged at about 8 to 12 weeks.

Suitability

Who Is HIFU Body Sculpting Suitable For?

HIFU works best on a pinchable layer of fat in someone who is not far above a healthy weight.

HIFU may suit you if

  • You have a stubborn area of fat on the belly or flanks that diet and exercise have not shifted
  • You are at or near a healthy weight; the studies reviewed used a BMI of 30 or less
  • The area has enough fat under the skin to treat safely, which your doctor checks
  • You prefer a non-surgical option and can wait about three months for the result

HIFU is not suitable, or needs care, if

  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • You have a wound, infection, rash or skin condition in the area
  • You have a hernia, metal implant or electronic device near the area; tell your doctor
  • Your main goal is weight loss; HIFU does not treat obesity

If you would like to lose weight overall first, our medical weight management programme is the place to start.

Procedure

What Happens During a HIFU Session

A session is done in the clinic and you can go home straight afterwards.

01

Assessment

Your doctor reviews your health, pinches and measures the area to check the fat thickness, and takes photos.

02

Marking

The treatment zone is marked in a grid so the energy is spread evenly.

03

Ultrasound Passes

Gel is applied and the handpiece is moved over each square in passes. You may feel heat, tingling or a deep ache, and the energy can be adjusted.

04

After the Session

The gel is wiped off. Mild redness or tenderness is common and settles on its own.

05

Review

Measurements and photos are compared at about 8 to 12 weeks.

What the research shows

HIFU has been studied for body contouring for more than a decade. A review by Frank and Saedi found a consistent reduction in waist measurement of at least 2 cm after one treatment, with side effects limited to mild, short-lived discomfort, redness, bruising and swelling, and occasional tingling or firm lumps that settled within about four weeks. A 2025 systematic review of 45 studies in the Aesthetic Surgery Journal reported an average waist reduction of about 3 cm after a single session and fewer than 5 percent of patients with significant side effects, while noting that energy settings and patient selection still vary between clinics and that poor technique can cause burns. The results are real but modest, which is why HIFU suits fine-tuning rather than large changes.

Choosing between HIFU and cryolipolysis

Both treatments reduce fat in a chosen area over a few months and suit people close to a healthy weight. Cryolipolysis needs a fold of fat that can be drawn into the applicator, while HIFU is delivered with a flat handpiece and needs a minimum depth of fat so the focused energy stays within the fat layer, clear of the skin surface and the muscle beneath. Some people find one more comfortable than the other. Your doctor looks at the shape of the area, the thickness of the fat and your preferences, and may suggest one, the other or a combination over time.

How to prepare

  • Tell your doctor about any medical conditions, implants, hernias or recent surgery near the area.
  • Try to keep your weight steady before treatment so the change is easier to measure.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and avoid applying creams or oils to the area on the day.

Aftercare

You can go back to normal activities straight away. If the area feels tender, ask your doctor which pain relief is safe for you. Mild swelling, bruising, tingling or small firm areas usually settle within a few weeks; contact the clinic if you notice blistering, a burn-like area, or pain that is getting worse. Keep up regular activity and balanced meals, because weight gain can hide the result.

What affects the cost of HIFU body sculpting?

The cost depends on the size and number of areas treated and whether a second session is planned. Treating the belly alone costs less than the belly and both flanks. Your doctor explains the plan and the total after your assessment.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How is HIFU different from cryolipolysis?
Both reduce a stubborn pocket of fat without surgery, and both work gradually. HIFU uses focused heat from ultrasound, while cryolipolysis uses controlled cooling. Your doctor recommends one or the other based on the area, the amount of fat and your skin.
How much fat does HIFU remove?
Results vary. In the studies reviewed by Frank and Saedi, the waist measurement was reduced by at least 2 cm after a single treatment, and one large trial found an average reduction of about 2.5 cm at 12 weeks. A 2025 review in the Aesthetic Surgery Journal reported an average of about 3 cm. Your doctor will explain what is realistic for you.
Does HIFU body sculpting hurt?
Most people feel heat, tingling or a deep aching during the passes, which stops when the handpiece moves on. The energy can be lowered if it is too uncomfortable. The area can feel tender or bruised for a few days to weeks.
How many HIFU sessions will I need?
Depending on the device, an area is treated in a single session made up of several passes or in a short course of sessions. After your review at about three months, you and your doctor can decide whether a further session would help.
Is it the same as HIFU face lifting?
No. Facial HIFU uses different devices and depths to tighten and lift the skin of the face and neck. Body sculpting targets the fat layer of the abdomen and flanks. The skin services page covers facial treatments.
Is there any downtime?
There is no hospital stay, and most people return to their normal routine the same day. Mild tenderness, swelling or bruising can last for a while, and a 2025 review found that fewer than 5 percent of patients had any significant side effects.
